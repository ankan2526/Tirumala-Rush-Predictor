Complete Tirumala Pilgrim Survival & Darshan Guide

Key timings, counter locations, dress codes, and compartment navigation

Vaikuntam Complex (VKC)

VKC 1 and VKC 2 house 49 compartments. Each compartment holds roughly 1,500–1,800 devotees. Once locked inside a compartment, release occurs in batches into the bridge corridor leading directly to the temple sanctum.

Free hot Annaprasadam, Sambhar rice, curd rice, hot milk, and drinking water are served continuously by Srivari Sevaks inside all compartments.
SSD Token Counters in Tirupati

Free Slotted Sarva Darshan (SSD) biometric tokens are issued daily at 3 major hubs in Tirupati down the hill:

  • Bhudevi Complex: Near Alipiri bus station.
  • Srinivasam Complex: Opposite Tirupati Central Bus Stand.
  • Govindaraja Choultries: Behind Tirupati Railway Station.
Tokens open around 4:00 AM – 5:00 AM and exhaust in 2 to 4 hours on peak days. Aadhaar card is strictly required.
Rules & Dress Code
  • Men: White Dhoti / Pancha with Kurta / Angavastram, or Pyjama with Kurta. Shirts allowed; jeans, shorts, and t-shirts strictly banned.
  • Women: Traditional Saree, Half-Saree, or Chudidar with Dupatta pinned. Western garments are not allowed.
  • Electronics: Mobile phones, smartwatches, cameras, and luggage must be deposited at free TTD locker counters.
